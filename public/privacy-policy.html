Privacy Policy

7 Seconds is a local multiplayer game available on the web and on Android via Google Play. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device with no server communication, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party services.

No account or login is required. No data leaves your device. No personal information is shared with third parties.

Data Collection

This app does not collect any data. There are no tracking tools, no cookies, and no telemetry of any kind.

Permissions

The Android app does not request any special permissions beyond what is required to run.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
