Privacy Policy

7 Seconds is a multiplayer strategy game available on the web and on Android via Google Play. The game can be played locally (on a single device) or online against other players or AI commanders. This policy explains what data is involved in each mode.

Local Play

When playing locally (Battle, vs AI, or Horde modes), the game runs entirely on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared. No server communication occurs.

Online Play

Online matches (against a friend, a random opponent or an AI commander, and Ranked) are played through our game server, hosted on Supabase. Matches are asynchronous: each player submits their plan for a round, and the server plays the round once both plans are in. The following data is processed:

Data processed during online play

DataPurposeStorage
Anonymous account (random user ID and session token) Identifies your device's player so you can only act in your own matches Created the first time you go online. The session is kept in your browser or app storage; the ID is stored on the server with your matches. It is not linked to your name, email or device.
Match data: the board, each round's plans (unit paths), results, and the time of each move Playing the match, letting you leave and come back, and showing the rounds you missed Stored on the server. Plans stay hidden from your opponent until both plans for a round are in. Only the two players of a match can read it. Unfinished matches end after 3–7 days without moves.
Display name (optional) Shown to your opponents Whatever you type under "Your name". It is stored on the server with each match you play.
Ranked profile (Android, optional): your Google Play Games player ID, display name and profile picture, your rating, wins, losses and rated results Ranked matchmaking, the Elo rating and the leaderboard Created only when you sign in to Ranked with Google Play Games. Your Play Games name, picture, rating and record are shown to other players (the leaderboard and your opponents). Your Google account's email address is not stored in your profile and never shown. You can delete the profile in the Ranked screen: unfinished ranked matches then count as losses, and your profile, ranked matches and devices are deleted.
Turn notification details (optional): a browser push subscription, an email address if you entered one, or an Android device token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) Telling you when a match needs your move Stored on the server only after you turn turn alerts on. Turning them off removes the push subscription or device token. Device tokens unused for 60 days are deleted.
IP address Delivering requests to and from the server Not stored by us. Supabase and Cloudflare may log it as part of standard infrastructure operations (see below).
Win/loss record against casual opponents Shown on the online screen Stored locally on your device only (localStorage).

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used only during online play:

  • Supabase — hosts the game server and database described above. Supabase's infrastructure (including Cloudflare edge servers) logs connection metadata such as IP addresses, retained for 1–7 days depending on plan tier. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Sign-In (Android, Ranked only) — verify your Play Games profile when you sign in to Ranked. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android, only with turn alerts on) and your browser's push service (web, only with notifications on) — deliver turn notifications to your device.

No analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry services are used.

Cookies and Local Storage

The game does not use cookies. It uses browser localStorage to store:

  • Game settings (sound, display preferences)
  • Your online session(s), your display name, and which online rounds you have already watched
  • Win/loss records against casual opponents
  • Your turn alert preference
  • Age verification status (whether you confirmed you are 13 or older)

You can clear it at any time through your browser or app settings. Clearing it signs this device out of its online matches.

Data Collection

No account or registration is needed: online play uses an anonymous account. Personal data is processed only as listed above, when you choose to play online, set a display name, sign in to Ranked, or turn on turn alerts.

Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area: processing for online matches is necessary to provide the online play you request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Your display name, Ranked profile and turn alerts are optional and processed on the basis of your choice to use them (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it by clearing the name, deleting your Ranked profile or turning alerts off. Infrastructure logs kept by Supabase and Cloudflare are based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access information about your data processing
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

You can delete your Ranked profile in the app. For anything else, such as removing your casual match history, contact the developer (below) with your match links.

Permissions

The Android app asks for permission to show notifications only if you turn turn alerts on.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. Online features require users to be 13 or older. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
