Privacy Policy

7 Seconds is a multiplayer strategy game available on the web and on Android via Google Play. The game can be played locally (on a single device) or online against another player. This policy explains what data is involved in each mode.

Local Play

When playing locally (Battle, vs AI, or Horde modes), the game runs entirely on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared. No server communication occurs.

Online Play

When you choose to play online, the game establishes a direct peer-to-peer (P2P) connection with the other player using WebRTC. This involves the following data processing:

IP address disclosure

Important: Online play uses peer-to-peer connections. This means your public IP address may be visible to the other player, and their IP address may be visible to you. This is an inherent property of WebRTC P2P connections. When a direct connection is not possible (e.g., on mobile networks), traffic is routed through a TURN relay server, in which case your IP address is visible to the relay provider but not to the other player. If you do not want your IP address exposed, do not use online play.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used only during online play:

  • Supabase — Provides the signaling relay for WebRTC connection setup. Signaling messages are ephemeral, but Supabase's infrastructure (including Cloudflare edge servers) logs connection metadata such as IP addresses and approximate geolocation. These logs are retained for 1-7 days depending on plan tier. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google and Cloudflare STUN servers — Used to determine your public IP address for NAT traversal during connection setup. These servers see your IP address but no game data.
  • Metered.ca TURN relay servers — When a direct P2P connection cannot be established (common on mobile/cellular networks), game traffic is routed through Metered's relay servers. In this case, Metered's servers process your IP address and relay encrypted game data between players. Game data remains encrypted (DTLS) in transit. Metered Privacy Policy.
  • freestun.net TURN relay — Used as a fallback relay server when direct connections fail. Functions the same as the Metered relay described above.

No analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry services are used.

Cookies and Local Storage

The game does not use cookies. It uses browser localStorage to store:

  • Game settings (sound, display preferences)
  • Player ID (random UUID, online play only)
  • Win/loss records (online play only)
  • Age verification status (whether you confirmed you are 13 or older)

All localStorage data stays on your device and is never transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Data Collection

This app does not intentionally collect personal data. No accounts, logins, or registrations are required. The only personal data processed is your IP address during online play, as described above.

Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area: the processing of IP addresses during online play is based on Legitimate Interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The IP address exchange is technically necessary for establishing a peer-to-peer connection and is initiated by the user choosing to play online. No IP addresses are logged or stored by this app. However, third-party infrastructure providers (Supabase, Cloudflare) may retain connection metadata including IP addresses in their standard operational logs, as described in the Third-Party Services section above.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access information about your data processing
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Since no personal data is stored on any server, there is nothing to access or delete server-side. You can clear locally stored data (player ID, scores) through your browser or app settings at any time.

Permissions

The Android app does not request any special permissions beyond what is required to run.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. Online features require users to be 13 or older. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
